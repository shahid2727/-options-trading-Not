V14.5 scanner stability fixes
- Stock bars pagination now catches page-level provider/timeouts and returns partial data instead of blocking the scan.
- Requests use separate connect/read timeouts.
- Bar-page telemetry now reports start/complete/failure, elapsed seconds, page number, rows and symbols.
- Scanner continues to per-symbol fallback/repair when multi-symbol bars fail.
- Telegram status exposes stage elapsed time and current bars page.
- Existing options filtering/scoring/alert logic is preserved.
